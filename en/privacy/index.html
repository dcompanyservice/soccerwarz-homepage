3. Advertising (planned)

Soccerwarz plans to offer optional rewarded advertisements through Google AdMob (for example, an ad you can choose to watch after a match to double the EXP you receive). An ad plays only when you tap the button for it, and the game can be played without watching any ads.

Google AdMob may collect and process device advertising identifiers and related information under Google's privacy policy. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, we will ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown. On iOS, we will ask whether you allow app tracking; if you do not, ads are shown without using the advertising identifier.

4. In-app purchases (planned)

We plan to offer a premium pass and character shard packs as in-app purchases. Payments are processed by Google Play or the App Store under their own terms and privacy policies. We receive only the information needed to verify and deliver your purchase.

5. Website

This website is a static information site. We do not ask you to create an account and we do not set cookies.

To understand how the site is used, your browser sends our server the address of each page you open, the kind of main button you press (for example, a store button or an email link), and, if you arrive from another site, the kind of site you came from (for example, a search engine or social media) or a campaign label in the link. These are counted only as daily totals per item. The page counter does not store IP addresses, cookies or device information.

Like any web server, our server records IP addresses in its access logs for security and troubleshooting. If you email us, we use your email address and message only to respond to you.

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as needed to operate the service:

  • Other players can see what you make public in the game, such as your nickname, profile icon, friend code, match results and the messages you send them.
  • Service providers who process information on our behalf (section 7).
  • App store platforms (Google, Apple) for downloads and, once available, payments and purchase verification.
  • The advertising provider (Google AdMob), once advertising is available, as described in section 3.
  • Authorities, when required by applicable law.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections between the game and our servers and restricted access to our database. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal information, to receive a copy of it, and to withdraw consent at any time. To make a request, contact us at the email address below with your friend code. We respond without undue delay, and within one month where the GDPR applies. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

11. Children

Soccerwarz is not directed at children under 13. Where the law requires the consent of a parent or legal guardian to process a child's personal information (for example, for children under 14 in Korea, or below the age set by each EU country), we will obtain that consent or will not process the information. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information without appropriate consent, please contact us and we will delete it.

12. International transfers

Our game servers and database are located in the Republic of Korea. If you use Soccerwarz from outside Korea, your information is transferred to Korea for processing. For users in the European Union, the European Commission has recognized Korea as providing an adequate level of data protection (adequacy decision of December 17, 2021). Once payments and advertising are available, the related information is also processed by Google and Apple, including in the United States.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy, including before the release of Soccerwarz. Each version is identified by the effective date at the top of this page. We will announce significant changes in the game or on this website before they take effect.
