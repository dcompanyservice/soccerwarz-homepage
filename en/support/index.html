Support

Need help with Soccerwarz? Start with the questions below.

Frequently asked questions

When will Soccerwarz be released?
Soccerwarz is in development for Android and iOS. Store links will be added to this website when the game is released.
Do I need to create an account?
No. The game signs you in as a guest automatically, using a random identifier created on your device. You do not need an email address or phone number.
I am changing to a new device. Will my progress move with me?
Your guest account is currently tied to the device it was created on. Please do not uninstall the game or clear its data if you want to keep your progress. A way to move your account to a new device is planned before release.
How do I delete my account and data?
See Delete your Soccerwarz account. Deletion requests are completed within 30 days.
Which languages are supported?
Korean, Japanese, English, Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, German, French, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese and Russian. The game follows your device language and can be changed in the settings.
I made a purchase but did not receive the item.
In-app purchases are not available yet. Once they are, please restart the game first; if the item still does not appear, contact us with your order number or receipt and your friend code.

Refunds

When in-app purchases become available, payments will be processed by Google Play or the App Store, and refund requests follow the policy of the store where you made the purchase:

  • Google Play refund policy
  • Request a refund from Apple

If a purchase is refunded, the currency and benefits granted by that purchase may be reclaimed, as described in the Terms of Service.

When you contact us, please include

  • Your friend code (8 characters, shown in the game) and nickname
  • Your device model and OS version (Android / iOS)
  • The app version, if you can find it
  • A description of the problem and, if possible, a screenshot
  • For purchase issues: the order number or receipt from Google Play or the App Store
