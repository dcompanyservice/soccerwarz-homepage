Soccerwarz Streaming & Video Guidelines

Effective date: November 18, 2026

Operated by: dcompany

Thank you for streaming and making videos about Soccerwarz!

Whether you are an individual creator or a company channel, you may create and publish Soccerwarz videos and live streams without asking for separate permission, as long as you follow these guidelines.

1. What you can do

  • Record, stream and edit Soccerwarz gameplay and publish it on YouTube, Twitch, Kick, TikTok, Instagram, X and similar platforms.
  • Monetize your videos and streams (ad revenue, donations, tips, memberships, Super Chats, etc.).
  • Use the official trailer and the screenshots, logo and key art from our press kit (https://soccer-warz.com/en/press/) in your videos and thumbnails.
  • Share honest opinions — guides, reviews, criticism. We never ask for positive-only coverage.

2. Please don't

  • Leak unreleased information: content from test servers, unreleased builds, datamining, or anything shared with you under a confidentiality agreement.
  • Imply an official sponsorship or partnership. Terms like "official stream" or "official partner" may only be used if agreed with dcompany in advance.
  • Promote or encourage cheating, exploits, hacks or macros, or sell accounts or coupon codes.
  • Infringe others' rights (music, footage, likeness) or break the law or your platform's rules.
  • Re-upload Soccerwarz videos without changes and present them as your own (using the official trailer to introduce the game is fine).

3. Disclosure (important)

If you receive anything of value from dcompany — such as a coupon code, in-game items or payment — please clearly disclose it so viewers can see it right away, in line with your local rules (for example, the US FTC Endorsement Guides).

  • Put the disclosure in the title or at the very beginning of the video/stream, e.g. "#ad", "Sponsored", "Gifted code from dcompany".
  • Avoid placing it only deep in the description or in hard-to-see text.
  • If your platform has a paid-promotion setting (e.g. YouTube's "Includes paid promotion"), please turn it on.

In Korea please use 「광고」 or 「협찬」, and in Japan 「広告」 or 「PR」.

If you play with a public coupon or without receiving anything from us, no disclosure is needed.

4. Random-reward items

Soccerwarz includes optional paid random-reward items (gacha). When you show pulls, please show the actual results as they are, so viewers are not misled.

5. Background music

The in-game background music is licensed from Composer Squad (Music by Composer Squad / composersquad.com). If your video receives a YouTube copyright claim (Content ID), email the video URL to contact@soccer-warz.com and we will help release it. If you prefer, you can turn off the background music in the game settings before recording (sound effects are fine to keep).

6. Other

  • We may ask you to edit or remove content that does not follow these guidelines.
  • We may update these guidelines with prior notice; changes will be posted on this page.
  • Platform rules and applicable laws take precedence over these guidelines.

7. Contact

Collaborations, code requests and questions about these guidelines: contact@soccer-warz.com
