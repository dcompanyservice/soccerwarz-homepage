Guide

7v7 grid soccer strategy: predict your rival and place your players to win — Launching Nov 18 · Android · iOS

How matches work

  • Set up — predict your opponent's plan and position seven players. A player in the goal area takes the goalkeeper role.
  • Face-off — players on the same tile contest the ball when it arrives.
  • Play — short and long passes, interceptions, duels and shots resolve automatically based on player stats.
  • Adapt — reposition or substitute between rounds. Win two of three rounds to take the match; tied matches go to a penalty shootout.

Formation tips

  1. Predict your opponent's strategy — put high-Steal players on the lanes they will use to carry the ball and you'll cut their passes.
  2. The player standing in the goal area becomes the goalkeeper. Pick someone with high Catch and Judge.
  3. If an opponent is on the tile where the ball lands, a one-on-one Battle starts. Put high-Battle players on the tiles where you receive the ball.
  4. Class matchups: Defense beats Attack, Tech beats Defense, Attack beats Tech. Check your opponent's main class and choose who faces them.
  5. Reposition between rounds to turn the game around. Win two of three rounds to take the match; ties go to a penalty shootout.
