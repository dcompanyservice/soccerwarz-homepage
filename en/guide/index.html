Soccerwarz Beginner Guide

7v7 grid soccer strategy: predict your rival and place your players to win — Launching Nov 18 · Android · iOS

How matches work

  • Set up — predict your opponent's plan and position seven players. A player in the goal area takes the goalkeeper role.
  • Face-off — players on the same tile contest the ball when it arrives.
  • Play — short and long passes, interceptions, duels and shots resolve automatically based on player stats.
  • Adapt — reposition or substitute between rounds. Win two of three rounds to take the match; tied matches go to a penalty shootout.

Formation tips

  1. Predict your opponent's strategy — put high-Steal players on the lanes they will use to carry the ball and you'll cut their passes.
  2. The player standing in the goal area becomes the goalkeeper. Pick someone with high Catch and Judge.
  3. If an opponent is on the tile where the ball lands, a one-on-one Battle starts. Put high-Battle players on the tiles where you receive the ball.
  4. Class matchups: Defense beats Attack, Tech beats Defense, Attack beats Tech. Check your opponent's main class and choose who faces them.
  5. Reposition between rounds to turn the game around. Win two of three rounds to take the match; ties go to a penalty shootout.

New to Soccerwarz? This guide covers everything you need for your first week — how to place players, how to read your opponent, and how to use your tickets and gems.

Start in 5 minutes

  • Finish the short tutorial — it walks you through placing players and your first real match.
  • Play your first PvP match. Every match costs 1 ticket, win or lose.
  • Open Rookie Missions ("Catch the Thief!", the thief icon on the main screen, app 1.2.3+). Clear 3 small missions a day for 7 episodes to earn gems, tickets and teammate shards.
  • Try Stage mode from the main screen. Clearing Stage 3 gives you a new character.

How to place players

  • The field is a 5×9 grid and each team fields 7 players. The player standing in your goal area becomes the goalkeeper.
  • You place your team at the same time as your opponent, without seeing their setup. Think about where they will attack.
  • Front row: shooters and players with high Battle — they receive the ball near the opponent's goal.
  • Middle: passers and high-Steal players to cut the lanes your opponent will use.
  • Back: a solid goalkeeper (high Catch and Judge) plus one or two defenders. A 3-defender wall is safe; an attacking setup scores faster but leaves gaps.

Read your opponent

  • A match is best of 3 rounds — win 2 rounds first to win the match.
  • Between rounds you can move up to 3 players. Look at where the last goal came from and block that lane.
  • Class matchups: Defense beats Attack, Tech beats Defense, Attack beats Tech. Put the right class against their main players.
  • If they keep shooting from the same side, stack Steal there next round.

Shootouts and goalkeepers

  • If the rounds are tied, the match goes to a penalty shootout. The 7 players on the field take turns, best shooters first.
  • Your keeper's Catch decides how many kicks are saved, so do not put a weak player in goal.
  • Each kicker has a favorite direction. Watching the opponent's kicks can help your keeper guess right.

Tickets, gems and summons

  • Tickets: 1 per match. They refill to 5 twice a day, and you can watch ads for more (up to 10 a day).
  • Gems: summons give character shards. 30 shards of the same character make that character, extra shards raise its stars.
  • Spend your first gems on summons to grow your team, and level up the players you actually use.
  • Coins from hunts and stories pay for level-ups.

FAQ

Is Soccerwarz free?
Yes. Soccerwarz is free to play on Android and iOS. Optional purchases include gems and a premium pass.
Do I need to win to clear missions?
No. "Play a match" missions count losses too. Only missions that say "win" need a win.
Why does my keeper never save penalties?
Penalty saves depend on your goalkeeper's Catch. Put a player with high Catch in your goal area.
How do I get more tickets?
Tickets refill twice a day, you can watch ads for extra tickets, and events and Rookie Missions also give tickets.
Can I play with friends?
Yes. You can team up with friends for 2v2 or 3v3, or join a club for club battles.
