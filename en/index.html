Soccerwarz

A soccer strategy game where your formation is the play.

Place seven pixel-art fantasy fighters on a 5 × 9 grid, reveal your formation at the same moment as your opponent, and watch passes, steals and battles play out from your players' stats.

How a match works

You make the decisions before the whistle. The match itself runs on your players' abilities.

1

Place

Both sides place a keeper and six field players on the grid at the same time, without seeing each other.

2

Reveal

Formations are revealed together. Players who share a tile with an opponent fight for the ball when it arrives.

3

Play

The ball moves on its own: short and long passes, steals, same-tile battles and shots, decided by stats.

4

Adjust

Between rounds, reposition or substitute players. Win two of three rounds; a draw goes to a penalty shootout.

Features

  • 7 vs 7 on a 5 × 9 gridA compact vertical pitch. Whoever stands in the goal area becomes the keeper, and every placement matters.
  • Best of three roundsEach round lasts up to 20 actions and ends when a goal is scored. Win two rounds to take the match; if it is still level, a penalty shootout decides it.
  • 35 charactersEvery character has its own type, trait and class. Classes work like rock-paper-scissors: attack beats tech, tech beats defense, and defense beats attack.
  • Grow your squadCharacters gain EXP from matches to level up, and character shards raise their stars.
  • Play your way1:1 matches against bots or online opponents, party play with friends, and guild wars (planned).
  • Free to playFree to download and play. A premium pass and shard packs are planned; drop rates will be shown in the game before any purchase.
  • 11 languagesKorean, Japanese, English, Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, German, French, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese and Russian.
