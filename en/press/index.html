About the game

Place your players strategically and battle!

Soccerwarz is a 7v7 soccer strategy game. Predict your opponent's strategy, place seven fantasy players on a 5 × 9 pitch, and watch the match play out: passes, steals and one-on-one battles are decided by each player's stats. Reposition mid-match to turn the game around, and settle draws in a penalty shootout.

Key features

  • Strategy firstRead your opponent and find the best formation for every round.
  • Fantasy squadKnights, goblins, sharks, minotaurs and more, each with its own stats and traits.
  • Comebacks and shootoutsReposition mid-match to turn things around. Draws go to a penalty shootout.
  • Ranked seasons and Club WarsClimb the season ranking and fight Club Wars together with your club.
