TAGLINE
Place your players strategically and battle!

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Soccerwarz is a 7v7 soccer strategy game. Predict your opponent's strategy,
place seven fantasy players on a 5 x 9 pitch, and watch the match play out:
passes, steals and one-on-one battles are decided by each player's stats.
Reposition mid-match to turn the game around, and settle draws in a penalty
shootout.

KEY FEATURES
- Strategy first: read your opponent and find the best formation for every round.
- Fantasy squad: knights, goblins, sharks, minotaurs and more, each with its own stats and traits.
- Comebacks and shootouts: reposition mid-match to turn things around. Draws go to a penalty shootout.
- Ranked seasons and Club Wars: climb the season ranking and fight Club Wars together with your club.
